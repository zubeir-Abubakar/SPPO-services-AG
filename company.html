PPO Services AG

Company

More than 70 years of produce expertise, international relationships and trusted commercial service.

History

Built around experience and long-term partnerships.

PPO Services AG has developed over decades through a strong focus on fruit and vegetable trading, trusted grower relationships and consistent customer service.

From its Swiss base, the company operates with a global perspective and a clear understanding of produce quality, seasonality and international supply needs.

Core values

  • Product quality and food safety
  • Reliable business partnerships
  • Responsible sourcing and sustainability
  • Swiss professionalism with international reach
